Online or downloaded? What is the difference
  • Online (this page): good for learning, practice and seeing if you like it. The page sends nothing, but your browser, its extensions and this connected device could see or keep what you type.
  • Downloaded and offline: the way to use it for real money. Check the file’s SHA-256 against the release page (the tool in the app’s TOOLS tab does this), then open it on a computer that is not on the internet.
  • Home Screen copy: after one visit online, a saved copy opens without a connection. When you are online it always loads the current version first, so a saved copy can be older. A saved page is not proof that your device is offline.
