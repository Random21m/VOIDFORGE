VOIDFORGE trial kit: do it once by hand, with a stopwatch

WARNING: THIS IS A PRACTICE RUN. Never use these words for real money. Destroy the sheets after. Use practice dice and cards only.

1. What to print

From vfmethod/, on Letter paper (A4 files in brackets):

  • forge-sheet.pdf (3 pages: sheets D, C and E) [A4: forge-sheet-a4.pdf]
  • bip39-binary-table.pdf (4 pages) [A4: bip39-binary-table-a4.pdf]

Print single-sided, at 100% (no "fit to page"). This is the 12-word version.

2. What you need

  • Two matching d6 (or one die thrown twice) and a cup
  • One 52-card deck
  • A pen
  • A timer or stopwatch
  • About 45 minutes, in one sitting

4. How to check your result

A. Reference script. Put your dice pairs (in the order thrown, including "again" pairs) and your cards (in dealt order, one string per deck) into a JSON file, for example mine.json:

{ "words": 12, "dice": "14 15 26 53 ...", "cards": ["7H KC TS 2D ...", "...", "..."] }

Run, from vfmethod/: node ref/forge.js mine.json [--md out.md] [--json out.json]. It prints the grids, marks and words to compare with your sheets. Or send me the photos or the values and I will run it.

B. Two real tools. Take words 1 to 11 and the 7 bits of row 12 of sheet E (offline devices; practice words only).

  • SeedSigner 0.8.7: Main menu > Tools > Calc 12th/24th word > 12 words > type words 1 to 11 > Build Final Word screen > Coin flip entropy > enter the 7 bits (Heads = 1, Tails = 0) > Next. It shows the 12th word and the fingerprint. For the address: Load seed > Done, then the seed's menu > Address explorer > Receive addresses.
  • VOIDFORGE: Word draw, same 11 words and 7 bits.

Compare: last word (both tools, and it must be in the 16-word table row from your 7 bits) / fingerprint / first address.
